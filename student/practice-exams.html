Six Exam Sets × Three Difficulty Levels

Exam 1A Standard is the recommended program baseline. A-level exams retain the original preparation difficulty. B-level exams are only a little harder. C-level exams are intentional mastery challenges and may be somewhat harder than the actual GISP-E examination.

Recommended use: establish readiness with A exams, use B exams to confirm deeper understanding, and use C exams as optional stress tests. A lower score at a higher difficulty is not automatically evidence of declining mastery.
