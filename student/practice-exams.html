GISCI-confirmed exam format: The GISP-E examination is multiple choice. Bucknell practice exams therefore remain multiple-choice and software-agnostic to mirror the examination format.

Practice Exam Library

Exam 1A Standard remains the recommended program baseline. The original 18 A/B/C exams remain available for baseline and progress practice. Two new 10-exam GISCI Companion series provide substantially stronger, independently authored practice aligned to GISCI's August 2026 guidance.

Recommended use: use the original A/B/C exams for baseline and progress checks; use the Calibrated / Right-Sized series for realistic full-length readiness practice; use the Advanced / Over-Preparation series later in preparation for deeper reasoning and stress testing. All exams are 75 multiple-choice questions with a 120-minute limit.
