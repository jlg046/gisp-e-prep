Getting to Know ArcGIS Pro 3.6 flipped-classroom pathway

Complete one chapter each week. Cumulative review sessions occur every third week and cover the three chapters completed in that block. The textbook supplies applied ArcGIS Pro practice, while the required ESRI training and GIS Book of Knowledge readings shown in the crosswalk close gaps needed for comprehensive GISP-E preparation.

How to use this pathway

  1. Complete the assigned chapter and exercises each week; attend the cumulative review session after every third chapter.
  2. Use the review session to resolve questions and connect software procedures to software-agnostic GIS concepts.
  3. Complete every supplement marked Required in the GIS Book of Knowledge crosswalk.
  4. Use practice-test results to add targeted ESRI training and BoK readings.

Important: The textbook is the spine of this pathway, not a complete substitute for all exam content. Ethics, law, organizations, advanced remote sensing, standards and several theoretical topics require supplements.
