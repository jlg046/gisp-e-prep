Chapter 4: Creating and editing spatial data

Sept 27–Oct 3 · Weekly flipped-classroom review session integrating the textbook, ESRI training and GIS Book of Knowledge.

Open Chapter 4 PDF

90-minute review plan

  1. Retrieval warm-up (10 min): students answer the core prompts independently.
  2. Textbook exercise debrief (20 min): compare workflows, decisions and errors from the chapter exercises.
  3. Software-agnostic concept bridge (20 min): connect ArcGIS Pro procedures to the assigned BoK concepts.
  4. Integrated case challenge (25 min): solve and defend the case below in small groups.
  5. Exam transfer and exit plan (15 min): answer new scenarios and select required remediation.

Discussion and retrieval prompts

  1. Design a geodatabase schema for a campus asset inventory, including geometry, keys, domains and topology rules.
  2. Differentiate positional, attribute, temporal, logical-consistency and completeness errors.
  3. Which editing controls prevent errors, and which errors still require validation after editing?

Integrated case

Multiple editors are building a trails database. Propose schema, domains, topology and quality-control rules that protect fitness for use.
