Recommended 90-minute cumulative review

  1. Retrieval warm-up (15 min): interleave key concepts from all three chapters.
  2. Workflow and misconception debrief (20 min): compare the chapter exercises and identify common errors.
  3. Software-agnostic concept bridge (20 min): connect ArcGIS Pro procedures to the aligned GIS Book of Knowledge concepts.
  4. Integrated case challenge (25 min): solve a scenario requiring concepts from multiple chapters.
  5. Exam transfer and remediation plan (10 min): identify remaining gaps and assign targeted ESRI/BoK review.

GISCI Readiness Check — Chapters 4–6

Use these as retrieval prompts—not as predicted exam questions. Try to answer each in your own words before looking at notes.

Questions you should be able to explain

  • Explain how a sound data model supports maintenance, quality, and analysis.
  • Evaluate whether a dataset is fit for a particular purpose using accuracy, completeness, currency, metadata, and resolution.
  • Explain why automated/repeatable workflows improve documentation and reproducibility.
  • Distinguish business needs from technical solutions when designing a GIS workflow or system.

Misconception check

  • Data modeling is not only a database administrator task.
  • More fields or more data do not automatically improve a GIS.
  • Purchasing better software does not by itself create an effective GIS.
