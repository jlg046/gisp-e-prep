GISCI Readiness Check — Chapters 1–3

Use these as retrieval prompts—not as predicted exam questions. Try to answer each in your own words before looking at notes.

Questions you should be able to explain

  • Explain why location and spatial relationships are fundamental to GIS.
  • Distinguish a geographic coordinate system from a projected coordinate system and explain projection tradeoffs.
  • Explain the difference between accuracy and precision and why uncertainty is unavoidable.
  • Explain why data organization, relationships, metadata, and data quality matter before analysis.

Misconception check

  • A digital map is not an exact representation of reality.
  • A technically successful GIS operation is not automatically a correct analytical answer.
  • More decimal places do not necessarily mean greater accuracy.
