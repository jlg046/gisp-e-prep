90-minute review plan

  1. Retrieval warm-up (10 min): students answer the core prompts independently.
  2. Textbook exercise debrief (20 min): compare workflows, decisions and errors from the chapter exercises.
  3. Software-agnostic concept bridge (20 min): connect ArcGIS Pro procedures to the assigned BoK concepts.
  4. Integrated case challenge (25 min): solve and defend the case below in small groups.
  5. Exam transfer and exit plan (15 min): answer new scenarios and select required remediation.

Discussion and retrieval prompts

  1. Match symbolization, classification, labeling and layout choices to audience, purpose and data type.
  2. What must be evaluated before sharing GIS content: accessibility, privacy, uncertainty, ownership and platform constraints?
  3. Apply professional ethics and organizational considerations to a map that could influence a consequential decision.

Integrated case

A public-facing equity map may affect funding. Design the map and sharing workflow while addressing classification, accessibility, privacy, ethics and governance.
