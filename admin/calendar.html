Fall 2026 Test Prep Calendar

The preparation window runs September 7 through November 15, immediately before the testing window opens November 16.

Complete one textbook chapter each week. The Baseline test occurs in Week 1, progress checks occur every other week after that, and cumulative textbook review sessions occur every third week.
