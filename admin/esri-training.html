Streamlined Esri training pathway

Program launch: September 7, 2026. Esri training provides applied practice; complete exam mastery also requires the workshops and GIS Book of Knowledge because the GISP-E is software agnostic.

Reduced workload: 17 core resources (33.8 known hours plus one duration to verify), 7 targeted resources, and 6 resources removed from the pathway.

Administrator guidance

Assign the core sequence universally. Use the targeted list only when diagnostics, workshop quizzes, or exam remediation identify a matching weakness. Do not assign the omitted resources as part of the standard pathway.

Removed from the streamlined pathway

ResourceReason
Creating a Map LayoutNarrow layout-production practice is already covered by Mapping & Visualization, the choropleth tutorial, and Workshop 1.
Teaching with GIS: Field Data Collection Using ArcGISFive-hour field-collection course is valuable operational training but too specialized for the exam-wide pathway.
Geostatistical Interpolation: Reaggregating Data Using Areal Interpolation (3.75 hours)Advanced areal-interpolation implementation is disproportionate to its blueprint coverage; retain the BoK/workshop concept instead.
Interpolating Points Using ArcGIS Online (1 hour)Duplicates interpolation and ArcGIS Online practice already provided elsewhere.
Geostatistical Interpolation: Creating a 3D Prediction Surface (3 hours)Specialized 3D prediction workflow is not needed universally; targeted 3D and interpolation resources remain available.
Get started with ModelBuilder in Map ViewerDuplicates ModelBuilder and ArcGIS Online concepts already covered by stronger core courses.
