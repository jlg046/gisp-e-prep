Streamlined ESRI training pathway

Program launch: September 7, 2026. ESRI training provides applied practice; complete exam mastery also requires the textbook pathway, cumulative review sessions every third week and GIS BoK because the GISP-E is software agnostic.

Administrator guidance

Assign the Core Pathway selectively based on prior coursework and diagnostic evidence. Use Advanced/Focused Trainings when textbook review sessions, progress checks or exam remediation identify a matching weakness.
