Six hands-on review workshops

The six sessions collectively cover every official blueprint domain. Each guide includes source-aligned BoK coverage, hands-on application, retrieval practice, and explicit links back to the practice-exam remediation workflow.

Suggested program rhythm

Use Exam 1 before Workshop 1; use Exams 2–3 during the middle of the series; reserve Exams 4–5 for targeted remediation after Workshops 4–6; use Exam 6 as the final comprehensive readiness check. Administrators should use the cohort dashboard between sessions to decide where to spend extra workshop time.

Practice materials are study aids; they are not official GISCI examination items.
