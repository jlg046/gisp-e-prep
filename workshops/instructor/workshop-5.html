Workshop 5: Cartography, Visualization & Applied GIS

Instructor guide

All workshops

Purpose

This workshop combines conceptual review, applied decision-making, retrieval practice, and direct GIS Book of Knowledge remediation.

Instructor facilitation

  1. Map critique: introduce the decision criteria, facilitate comparison of defensible alternatives, and close with software-agnostic takeaways.
  2. Redesign sprint: introduce the decision criteria, facilitate comparison of defensible alternatives, and close with software-agnostic takeaways.
  3. Application transfer: introduce the decision criteria, facilitate comparison of defensible alternatives, and close with software-agnostic takeaways.

Assessment guidance

Use a short retrieval quiz and require students to justify choices. Review cohort results before the next session and assign targeted remediation rather than repeating material universally.
